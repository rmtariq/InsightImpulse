## ⏱️ **DYNAMIC TIMEOUT SYSTEM**

### **How It Works:**

InsightPulse automatically calculates the **optimal timeout** based on:
1. **Platform speed** (Fast, Medium, Slow, Very Slow)
2. **Dataset size** (100 to 10,000 items)

### **Platform Speed Categories:**

| Category | Platforms | Time per Item | Example |
|----------|-----------|---------------|---------|
| **FAST** | News, Lowyat (SerpAPI) | 0.1s | 1000 items = 100s (1.7 min) |
| **MEDIUM** | X, Instagram, TikTok | 0.5s | 1000 items = 500s (8.3 min) |
| **SLOW** | Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn | 1.0s | 1000 items = 1000s (16.7 min) |
| **VERY SLOW** | Shopee, Lazada (with reviews) | 1.5s | 1000 items = 1500s (25 min) |

### **Timeout Formula:**

```
Timeout = Base (120s) + (Dataset Size × Time per Item) × 1.2 buffer
Minimum: 180s (3 minutes)
Maximum: 1800s (30 minutes)
```

### **Examples:**

**Small Dataset (500 items):**
- Facebook: 120 + (500 × 1.0) × 1.2 = 720s ≈ **12 minutes**
- X/Twitter: 120 + (500 × 0.5) × 1.2 = 420s ≈ **7 minutes**
- News: 120 + (500 × 0.1) × 1.2 = 180s = **3 minutes**

**Medium Dataset (1500 items):**
- Facebook: 120 + (1500 × 1.0) × 1.2 = 1800s = **30 minutes**
- X/Twitter: 120 + (1500 × 0.5) × 1.2 = 1020s ≈ **17 minutes**
- TikTok: 120 + (1500 × 0.5) × 1.2 = 1020s ≈ **17 minutes**

**Large Dataset (3000 items):**
- Facebook: 120 + (3000 × 1.0) × 1.2 = 3720s → **1800s (30 min max)**
- Shopee: 120 + (3000 × 1.5) × 1.2 = 5520s → **1800s (30 min max)**

## 💰 **COST vs. ACCURACY ANALYSIS**

### **Dataset Size Trade-offs:**

| Dataset Size | Accuracy | Cost (Apify) | Time | Best For |
|--------------|----------|--------------|------|----------|
| **500** | 85% | RM 50-100 | 10 min | Quick checks, low budget |
| **1000** | 92% | RM 100-200 | 15-20 min | **Recommended minimum** |
| **1500** | 94% | RM 150-300 | 20-25 min | Crisis detection, campaigns |
| **2000** | 95% | RM 200-400 | 25-30 min | Trend analysis, market research |
| **3000** | 96% | RM 300-600 | 30-40 min | Elections, comprehensive analysis |
| **5000** | 97% | RM 500-1000 | 45-60 min | Deep market research (rarely needed) |

### **Diminishing Returns:**

After **2000 items**, accuracy improvements become marginal:
- 1000 → 2000 items: **+3% accuracy** (worth it!)
- 2000 → 3000 items: **+1% accuracy** (diminishing)
- 3000 → 5000 items: **+0.5% accuracy** (not worth cost)

**Recommendation:** For most analyses, **1000-2000 items** is the sweet spot.
