- ✅ **Dashboard Access** - View insights, trends, reports
- ✅ **API Access** - Integrate with their systems
- ✅ **Data Credits** - Monthly quota for crawling

- ✅ **Export Capability** - CSV, Excel, API endpoints

### **What User Does NOT Get:**

- ❌ Infrastructure access
